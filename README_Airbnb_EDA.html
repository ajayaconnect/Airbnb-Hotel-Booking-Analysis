01 — Project Overview
What This Analysis Answers

This project analyses the Airbnb NYC 2019 dataset to understand what drives pricing, which neighbourhoods dominate supply and demand, how different room types perform, and what patterns exist in host behaviour and guest engagement. The insights are structured to serve three audiences: hosts looking to price competitively, investors identifying high-return locations, and Airbnb's strategy team targeting growth markets.

09 — Business Conclusion
Strategic Takeaways
For Hosts & Investors

Manhattan offers the highest average returns ($146/day) but faces intense competition. Brooklyn's Williamsburg and Bed-Stuy provide a strong price-to-demand balance. Queens and the Bronx represent underserved growth markets with room for new listings.

For Airbnb Strategy

A small number of hosts dominate the market (Michael, David, John). The majority of demand is for 1–2 night short stays. Entire home/apt listings are near-equally popular with private rooms — both segments should be nurtured to maintain listing diversity.
